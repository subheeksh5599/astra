the gap

A transfer can leave one chain
and never arrive on the other.

The tokens are burned on the source chain. The destination has not minted them, and it will not, until somebody hands it the signed attestation. Until then the money is in flight: not lost, not spendable, and invisible unless someone is watching both ends.

the rail
invariant

One burn, exactly one mint.

For every transfer the source chain announced, the destination chain received it once, or it has not received it yet, and there is no third possibility. ASTRA reads both ends and proves the pair from the chains themselves. It keeps no ledger of its own that could disagree with them.

prove it: scripts/prove_pairing.py, which exits non-zero the moment a burn has no mint

Observe

The source chain's own log stream says which transfers exist. The destination's own answer says which ones arrived. No indexer is asked what is pending.

Defer or deliver

An attestation that is not signed yet is not an error, so the rail waits and says so. An attestation that is signed is handed to the destination contract, which decides whether the transfer may complete.

Refuse by name

Every refusal carries the reason code and the evidence that produced it, taken from the chain or from the destination's own revert, never from a guess.

when money may not move

The refusal is the product.

ReasonWhat it meansRead from
ALREADY_DELIVEREDThe destination received this transfer already. A second delivery would spend gas to fail, or worse, double-send under a local nonce bookkeeping system.destination revert
CALLER_RESTRICTEDThe message names a single address allowed to complete it. If that is not the executor, completion is not ours to attempt, and the rail names who can.message field
ATTESTATION_PENDINGThe source chain has not finalised, so no attestation is signed. Nothing is wrong; it is early. The rail waits.attestation service
RECIPIENT_MISMATCHThe transfer being asked about is not the transfer in the message: a different recipient, amount, or token. A request is not evidence.message body
ROUTE_MISMATCHThe message travels between domains other than the ones the caller named.message header
MESSAGE_INCONSISTENTOur decode of the message and the attestation service's decode disagree. A decision taken on a disagreement is a decision about a transfer nobody has described correctly, so the rail stops.two decodes
UNSUPPORTED_DOMAINThe transfer goes to a chain this rail does not watch, and an unwatched chain has no contract to call.rail config
WRONG_TRANSMITTERThe contract about to be called reports a different chain domain than the message's destination. The call would be aimed at the wrong contract.on-chain read
PREFLIGHT_REVERTThe destination would reject the delivery for some other reason. The rail carries that reason rather than replacing it.destination simulation
how it executes

Nothing here signs with a key of its own

The rail holds no wallet. Every state-changing call goes through an execution layer that simulates first, reports whether the call would revert, and only then broadcasts, with an idempotency key derived from the transfer itself. The rail reads the destination's verdict out of that simulation before anything is spent.

The payer side stays in your wallet

Creating a transfer is your transaction: you approve and you burn, from your own address, and the message names you as the recipient. The rail's job starts the moment your transfer is in flight and stops when the destination has it.
