Cross-chain delivery rail

A transfer can leave one chain
and never arrive on the other.

The tokens are burned on the source chain. The destination has not minted them, and it will not, until somebody hands it a signed attestation. In between, the value is spendable nowhere.

The rail reads both chains and answers with a decision. Nothing is broadcast here.

The invariant

One burn, exactly one mint.

For every message the source chain announced, the destination chain received it once, or it has not received it yet, and there is no third possibility. The rail reads both ends and proves the pair from the chains themselves.

Executable

prove_pairing.py

Reads both chains and reports, per transfer, paired, in flight, stranded or unwatched. It exits non-zero the moment a burn is stranded with a signed attestation, or a nonce was received twice.

Why it holds

Nothing is taken on trust

The source chain's own log stream says which transfers exist. The destination contract's own record says which ones arrived. No indexer is asked what is pending, and nothing this rail wrote down is consulted.

When money may not move

The refusal is the product.

Every refusal carries a reason code and the evidence that produced it, taken from the chain or from the destination's own answer.

ReasonWhat it meansRead from
ALREADY_DELIVEREDThe destination received this transfer already. A second delivery spends gas to fail, or double-sends under a local nonce bookkeeping system.the destination's record
CALLER_RESTRICTEDThe message names a single address allowed to complete it. If that is not the executor, completion is not ours to attempt, and the rail names who can.message field
ATTESTATION_PENDINGThe source chain has not finalised, so no attestation is signed. Nothing is wrong; it is early. The rail waits.attestation service
RECIPIENT_MISMATCHThe transfer being asked about is not the transfer in the message: a different recipient, amount or token. A request is not evidence.message body
ROUTE_MISMATCHThe message travels between domains other than the ones the caller named.message header
MESSAGE_INCONSISTENTOur decode of the message and the attestation service's decode disagree, so the rail stops instead of deciding about a transfer nobody has described correctly.two decodes
UNSUPPORTED_DOMAINThe transfer goes to a chain this rail does not watch, and an unwatched chain has no contract to call.rail configuration
WRONG_TRANSMITTERThe contract about to be called reports a different chain domain than the message's destination.on-chain read
PREFLIGHT_REVERTThe destination would reject the delivery for another reason. The rail carries that reason rather than replacing it.destination simulation
How it executes

No key, no signing, no guessing.

Execution

Simulate, then broadcast

Every state-changing call goes through the execution layer: simulate, read the verdict, broadcast with an idempotency key derived from the transfer, then poll until the hash appears.

Payer side

Your wallet signs the burn

Creating a transfer is the payer's own transaction. They approve and they burn from their own address, and the message names them as the recipient.

Record

A receipt per decision

Deliveries and refusals are written to the same folder with the evidence that produced them. The pair of them is the story: the delivery is why the refusal is correct.
